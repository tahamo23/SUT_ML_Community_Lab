## 🕒 Chronological Order of Events

### 1. Chapter Introduction (15:00 – 15:10)

The session commenced with an introduction to Chapter 5 of *Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras & TensorFlow*, focusing on Support Vector Machines (SVMs).

**Presenter:** Hamed Mirzaei

**Topic:** *Chapter 5: Support Vector Machines*

**Overview:** The introduction provided a general overview of Support Vector Machines, their applications in machine learning, and the main concepts that would be covered throughout the chapter. In addition, a review of fundamental linear algebra concepts was presented to establish the mathematical foundations required for understanding Support Vector Machines and their underlying optimization methods.

The linear algebra discussion covered the mathematical concepts and tools commonly used in SVM formulations, providing participants with the necessary background for understanding vectors, decision boundaries, and the mathematical operations involved in SVM algorithms.
